Pill Box

  • Drag to aim, let go to drop. Tap ⟳ to turn the pill.
  • Whenever two pills of the same color touch, they clone a new pill. Each pair only clones once.
  • Each level has a mix color. Touch its two ingredient colors together and they burst into dust, wiping out nearby pills too.
  • Chain reactions: any ingredient pill touching a crumbling pill crumbles too, and fresh mixes while it settles stack a combo multiplier.
  • Later levels hide the recipe, then the color itself. Find it by luck.
  • Don't let pills spill or pile over the rim.
